05 — FAQ

Common questions.

Demo bilkul free hai? +

Yes. We build a working demo first — you review it on your phone and request changes. Payment is only requested after you approve the final result.

Kitne time mein delivery hoti hai? +

Standard projects ship in 4–7 days. Applications and complex systems are scoped with a milestone timeline before work begins.

Koi monthly fee ya commission? +

No commission, ever. You get an optional low-cost hosting/maintenance plan after launch — or you can host it yourself; the choice is yours.

Payment kaise karni hoti hai? +

50% advance after demo approval, 50% before go-live. EasyPaisa, JazzCash and bank transfers are all accepted.

Kya main khud edits kar sakta hoon? +

Absolutely. Every store ships with a plain editable config file — prices, products, WhatsApp number and hours are all one line each. We also handle edits for you free during the support window.

Support ke baad kya hota hai? +

After the free support window you can opt into a maintenance retainer, or simply keep the site running — static builds rarely need changes.
